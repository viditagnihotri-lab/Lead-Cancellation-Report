Dashboard Guide
How to read this dashboard
Compares what the sales team logged as the cancellation reason against what the sprint trail actually shows. The goal is to find patterns, improve data quality, and identify where a different action could have changed the outcome.
Reason Quality Does the logged reason match what the sprint trail shows?
Accurate
The reason logged matches what the sprint notes show. Example: "Client not responding" with a sprint trail showing multiple unanswered calls and no engagement.
Needs Review
The reason doesn't match the sprint trail — or gives zero information. Examples: "Other" (no signal), "Cost higher than qualification" but client was approving layouts the day before, "Timeline Misalignment" but comment reveals a legal scope issue. Not a blame tag — flags where data needs a second look.
Sprint Quality Is the sprint trail complete enough to understand what happened?
Complete
Meaningful entries with descriptions — CBM notes, follow-up logs, meeting outcomes. You can read the entire lead journey.
Gaps Present
Missing entries, long silences, or only priority flags with no descriptions. Doesn't mean nothing happened — means it wasn't logged, so we can't learn from it.
Recovery Signal Was there a moment where a different action could have changed the outcome?
Signal Present
A visible intervention point existed — cost concern never resolved, client said "I'll call back" and team never followed up, proposal sent with no follow-up sprint. Doesn't mean the loss was definitely avoidable — it means the data shows a moment worth reviewing.
No Signal
Outcome driven by external factors — BTS construction, legal scope issue, operational infeasibility. These are correct exits. Decision-making was sound.
Dead-Zone GapDays between last sprint and cancellation date
Most direct measure of follow-up discipline. A high gap = lead was mentally dropped before it was formally cancelled.
0–15d
Healthy
Lead was actively managed close to cancellation
16–60d
Attention needed
Follow-up cadence broke down — review why contact stopped
60d+
Zombie lead
Lead abandoned in pipeline — formal closure delayed by months
